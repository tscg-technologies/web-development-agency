Terms & Conditions

Standard terms of engagement, service parameters, and usage rules governing WEB DEVELOPMENT AGENCY.

Last Updated: January 2026 | Effective Date: Immediate

1. Acceptance of Terms

By accessing and navigating this website, you acknowledge that this is a generic demonstration platform created to represent the design, engineering capabilities, and portfolio execution of WEB DEVELOPMENT AGENCY. If you engage our agency for custom web development services, separate written Statements of Work (SOW) and service level agreements govern project deliverables.

2. Intellectual Property & Code Ownership

Unless otherwise agreed in a customized Master Services Agreement:

  • Agency Frameworks: General design structures, reusable vanilla utility libraries, and boilerplate templates created independently by WEB DEVELOPMENT AGENCY remain the intellectual property of the agency.
  • Client Deliverables: Upon receipt of complete payment, all bespoke frontend code, custom graphical compositions, approved layouts, and customer-provided copy transition to 100% client ownership.
  • No Vendor Lock-in: Client projects are built with clean, standards-compliant HTML5, CSS3, and modern vanilla JavaScript with no proprietary runtime locks or encrypted obfuscation.

3. Scope of Web Engineering Services

Each commercial development engagement proceeds according to the agency's 5-stage project framework:

  1. Discovery & Strategy: Architectural analysis, wireframing, sitemap drafting, and technical specification sign-off.
  2. UI/UX Prototyping: High-fidelity responsive mockups in Figma, design system definitions, and design approvals.
  3. Frontend & CMS Development: Semantic coding, CSS responsive styling, interaction programming, and CMS setup.
  4. QA & Cross-Browser Testing: Verification across Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge, iOS, and Android screen viewports.
  5. Staging, Deployment & Handover: Production deployment on client hosting (e.g. Hostinger, VPS, or cloud CDN), DNS configuration, and 30-day post-launch warranty.

4. Quotations and Statements of Work

Estimates generated via our interactive 5-Step Project Planner or listed on our Pricing page reflect typical scope parameters. Official, binding project proposals are provided in writing following technical discovery and are valid for 30 calendar days from issuance. Scope additions requested after sign-off are processed through transparent Change Order requests.

5. Client Responsibilities

Smooth project execution relies on mutual collaboration. Clients agree to:

  • Supply company logos, approved copy, brand assets, and product catalog data according to agreed milestone schedules.
  • Provide access credentials (hosting control panels, domain DNS managers, or third-party API accounts) when deployment services are included.
  • Provide consolidated, timely feedback on prototype deliverables within 5 business days of submission.

6. Limitation of Liability

WEB DEVELOPMENT AGENCY engineers websites to the highest standards of code reliability, accessibility, and speed. However, the agency shall not be held liable for losses resulting from:

  • Third-party web host downtime, DNS provider routing failures, or upstream telecommunications outages.
  • Modifications made to source code, plugins, or server configurations by third parties after client handover.
  • Search engine algorithm changes impacting external search rank positions over time.
