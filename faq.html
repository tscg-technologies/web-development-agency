Frequently Asked Questions.

Find answers to common questions about our website development process, technical architecture, e-commerce capabilities, pricing scopes, and ongoing support.

1. General Questions

You can begin by navigating to our "Start a Project" page and completing the 5-step project brief form. Alternatively, send us an enquiry through the Contact page. We review your requirements and follow up with a structured scope of work.

We typically require: an overview of your business model, your desired sitemap (pages required), any brand assets (logos, high-res photos, color preferences), and descriptions of your core services. If you need assistance structuring content, we guide you during the Discovery phase.

4. Pricing, Timelines & SEO

Every website project has unique scope requirements. Pricing depends directly on the number of unique page layouts, feature complexity (e.g., e-commerce, custom app dashboards), and content integration needs. We provide transparent custom quotations after reviewing your brief.

Timelines depend on scope and feedback velocity. A focused 3-page starter website can typically be completed in 2 to 3 weeks, while comprehensive multi-page corporate or e-commerce platforms typically require 4 to 8 weeks through discovery, design, development, and testing.
